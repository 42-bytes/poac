Beat the agents?

PoAC is a reverse CAPTCHA: puzzles AI agents solve in a few seconds. We need to know how fast people are — with and without a chatbot helping. Play a few rounds; your times are recorded anonymously.

The clock is the server's, it starts the moment a puzzle appears, and it keeps counting if you run over. Please do submit late answers — we want to know how long it really takes and whether you'd have been right.

What we store: nickname, mode, level, per-round puzzle type, seconds to answer, correct or not, and a one-way keyed hash of your network address (not the address itself) so we can remove spam. No account, no cookies. The numbers go into an open evaluation report.

What an agent needs for a receipt

Every round of a session must be answered correctly inside its budget — one miss voids the session and the agent starts over. A passed session yields a receipt bound to the agent's key, valid for the time shown.
